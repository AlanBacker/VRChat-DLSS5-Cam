    VRChat DLSS5 Cam

    A free Windows app that runs the VRChat camera, your photos and your videos through NVIDIA DLSS 5 and saves the result.

    What it does

    VRChat DLSS5 Cam takes a picture and runs it through NVIDIA DLSS 5 Neural Rendering. A neural network renders the light, the skin and the materials again. You see the result next to the original, and save it as a file.

    • Live from VRChat. The VRChat camera sends its picture to the app. Press Ctrl+Alt+P and a lossless PNG is saved, while VRChat stays in front.
    • Photos and videos. Open a screenshot or a recording, adjust the look, and save it as PNG, MP4, GIF, APNG or WebP.
    • Many files at once. Every file you open goes into the library. Process a few of them, or all, in one run.
    • Safe to use. The app does not touch VRChat and needs no mod. Your pictures never leave your computer.

    The guide

    Short steps from the download to your first saved picture, and on to the live camera, videos and batches. Each takes a few minutes.

    1. 1InstallPick the download for your graphics card, unzip it and start the app.
    2. 2First pictureOpen a photo, compare it with the DLSS 5 result and save it. VRChat does not need to run.
    3. 3Live cameraSend VRChat's camera to the app, watch DLSS 5 live and save shots with a hotkey.
    4. 4VideosOpen a video or an animated GIF, choose the part you want and save it with DLSS 5 applied.
    5. 5LibraryCollect files, process many at once, give single files their own values, and turn or crop them.
    6. 6PresetsKeep the looks you like under a name, and step back through every change.
    7. 7SavingChoose the folder and the file names, keep transparency, and find the app's own files.
    8. 8UpdatesHow the app finds and installs a new version, and what to do where GitHub is slow.
